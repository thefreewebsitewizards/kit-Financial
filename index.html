About Kit Batina

A Unique Perspective on Financial Planning

Kit Batina holds a master’s degree in Health Services Management & Policy from George Washington University in Washington, DC and a bachelor’s degree in Economics from UC San Diego. As a former nursing home administrator, she has witnessed the financial devastation of long term care expenses and has dealt with this issue in her personal life with her own family. She has worked politically with Senate committees and healthcare advocacy groups in Washington, DC regarding elder care/aging issues and universal healthcare. She is committed to educating people about little-known options to preserve their money from the exorbitant cost of long term care. Contrary to popular opinion, one need not spend down to the Medicaid limits to qualify for Medicaid for home care, assisted living or nursing home care.

Kit Batina is a financial services representative specializing in college planning, retirement and long term care planning. These services go hand-in-hand as many people forgo retirement planning in order to fund college expenses for their children. In addition, even the best retirement plan can be upended in the event long-term care is needed. There are strategies that offer solutions to these concerns balancing all three while providing the best possible retirement ensuring your money lasts longer than you do.

All consultations are free of charge and individually designed with your specific needs and goals in mind.
